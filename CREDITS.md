## Audio
- **Interface Sounds**, **Music Jingles** by [Kenney](https://www.kenney.nl) - License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  8 clips converted to mono 32 kHz MP3 and volume-normalised (`public/sfx/`); the originals are not redistributed.
  If a clip fails to load the game falls back to synthesised Web Audio tones.
- **RPG Audio** and **Interface Sounds** by [Kenney](https://www.kenney.nl) - License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  14 clips (footsteps, book, pot, coins, door, cloth…) converted to mono 32 kHz MP3 and loudness-normalised for the 3D room (`public/sfx/room/`).
